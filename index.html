TorchDice Power Generator

This application allows users to roll from a list of over 1k superpowers and then add modifiers that further add complexity. Modifiers, terms, and categories all have detailed explanations of what they entail for how the power can be expressed. All the terms and powers come from the trpg system; TorchDice.
